# Research Track 01 — Adult Audio

## Working hypothesis

A creator-first adult audio discovery and subscription platform may offer unusually attractive economics because delivery is cheap, repeat listening is natural, and the format supports creators, series, follows, tips and subscriptions.

## Current anchors

- Quinn: creator-led model, subscription + virtual gifts.
- Dipsea: professionally produced subscription catalogue.
- Adjacent products: Bloom, Ferly and romance/audio-fiction apps.

## Questions to answer next

1. Market size and growth.
2. User demographics and use cases.
3. Creator economics.
4. Discovery pain.
5. Web vs app distribution.
6. Payment/app-store constraints.
7. Content acquisition.
8. MVP feature set.
9. SEO/discovery opportunity.
10. Route to first €10k/month.

## Current product sketch

Feed → audio → creator → series → follow → save → premium/tip.

Do not assume AI voice should be the brand. Treat AI as a possible production tool unless research shows users actively want AI-native voices/characters.
