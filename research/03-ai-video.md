# Research Track 03 — AI Video

## Working hypothesis

"AI porn" is already too broad and too easy to copy. The more interesting product is a synthetic-only adult video network in which persistent fictional creators/characters become the identity layer.

Possible graph:

Creator → Fictional Character → Universe/Series → Videos → Followers

## Product principles to investigate

- synthetic-only
- no real-person impersonation
- no non-consensual deepfakes
- persistent fictional identities
- creator pages
- character pages
- series
- follows
- short-form discovery
- long-form pages
- provenance / tool metadata where useful

## Main research risks

- content moderation
- age assurance
- payment processors
- hosting/CDN acceptance
- storage/transcoding economics
- model/content rights
- deepfake detection / identity verification
- commodity supply overwhelming discovery quality

## Core moat hypothesis

The asset must be the creator/character/follower graph and taste data, not the generated video files themselves.
