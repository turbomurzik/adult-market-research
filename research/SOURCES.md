## Evidence policy

- Separate first-party facts from modeled estimates.
- Record source date where available.
- Traffic-provider estimates are directional, not audited.
- Revenue estimates from private-company data services are directional unless corroborated.
- Prefer first-party pricing/product pages for current product mechanics.
- Preserve contradictory estimates instead of silently selecting the one that fits the thesis.
