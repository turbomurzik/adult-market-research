# FURRY-R01 — Reconciliation Note

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Status:** Canonical reconciliation of two independent furry research passes  
**Scope:** Adult Audio only

---

## 1. Why this note exists

The first FURRY-R01 pass and an independent second pass broadly agree that:

- specialized adult furry audio is worth testing;
- Furry Explicit is better supported by direct evidence than Furry Worlds;
- Furry Worlds may have stronger long-term IP/retention upside if serialized audio demand is real;
- the market is fragmented and should not be sized from total furry traffic.

The second pass, however, introduced several strategically important refinements that change the canonical validation plan.

---

## 2. Major refinement: Explicit-C vs Explicit-K

The original Furry Explicit hypothesis was too broad.

The independent pass identified two different attachment models:

### Explicit-K — category anchored

The user searches by:
- species;
- pairing;
- kink;
- dynamic;
- scenario;
- POV.

This is useful for discovery but is easy to commoditize and faces strong free/AI substitution.

### Explicit-C — character anchored

The user returns for a **specific recurring original character**:
- stable voice;
- stable visual identity;
- recognizable personality;
- repeat drops;
- light continuity;
- optional progression without requiring a full narrative world.

This model sits between generic explicit roleplay and Furry Worlds.

### Reconciled thesis

> **Category may drive acquisition; character may drive retention.**

Explicit-C is therefore the current primary furry hypothesis.

---

## 3. Major refinement: market-size posture

The second pass is more conservative than the first.

Important corrections:

- huge Fur Affinity / e621 traffic is not furry-audio TAM;
- strong furry porn consumption is not willingness-to-pay;
- strongest furry adult WTP evidence is often in games/VNs, not pure audio;
- visible furry-audio creators remain small compared with mainstream adult-audio leaders;
- 2–4k paying users would already represent meaningful penetration of the plausible specialist market.

Therefore furry audio should be treated as a **small, fragmented niche until direct validation proves otherwise**.

---

## 4. Major refinement: economics

The unit-economics script makes the scale requirement explicit.

Under current assumptions:

- €7.99–9.99 subscription pricing;
- adult payment costs;
- taxes;
- refunds/chargebacks;
- age verification;
- content production;

roughly **2,000–4,000 payers** may be required for approximately **€10k/month operating profit before CAC**.

At 15% monthly churn that means roughly **300–600 new payers every month just to stay flat**.

This makes retention and acquisition efficiency central, not secondary.

---

## 5. Major refinement: Gate 0 before production

The second pass identifies payment/compliance and creator feasibility as potential blockers that should be resolved before expensive content production.

### FURRY-G00A — Creator gate

Approx. 15 structured creator/VA/project conversations:
- actual sales where shareable;
- licensing willingness;
- back-catalogue availability;
- rev-share expectations;
- exclusivity;
- interest in multi-creator aggregation.

### FURRY-G00B — Processor gate

Written guidance from at least two adult-friendly processors:
- fictional adult anthro content;
- allowed/prohibited themes;
- age-assurance requirements;
- creator documentation;
- reserves/chargebacks;
- jurisdiction restrictions.

If both processor routes reject the intended content policy, furry commercial validation stops before meaningful production spend.

---

## 6. Where we do NOT adopt the independent pass literally

The independent pass proposes testing Furry Worlds as a series inside the Explicit-C catalogue.

We do **not** canonize that consumer-facing merge.

Reason:

- Worlds is story-first;
- Explicit is immediate-fantasy-first;
- mixing them can contaminate the test;
- the project's existing brand principle is one primary intent per consumer brand.

Therefore Worlds and Explicit may share:
- backend;
- analytics;
- production;
- operational tooling;

but their validation façades remain separate.

---

## 7. Reconciled priority order

### 1. Explicit-C — primary

Test:
- repeat visits to same character;
- character landing vs category landing;
- payment intent;
- cross-character exploration.

### 2. Furry Worlds — exploratory

Test:
- episode completion;
- next-episode intent;
- season-pass intent;
- character/world follow behavior.

### 3. Explicit-K — control / secondary

Use to test whether category-led discovery is enough by itself or merely an acquisition layer for character-led retention.

---

## 8. What remains unknown

- real furry-audio churn;
- actual creator sales volumes across itch/Gumroad/Patreon;
- creator willingness to aggregate;
- processor interpretation of anthro-specific edge cases;
- real CAC;
- character vs category conversion;
- pure-audio conversion from furry VN/fiction audiences;
- optimal story/explicit ratio for Worlds.

---

## 9. Portfolio decision after reconciliation

**Furry is now HOLD — interesting but not compelling.**

The research remains valid and preserved, but the project will not currently spend the next validation slot on:
- creator interviews;
- processor outreach;
- content production;
- smoke tests;
- platform work.

If the branch is reactivated later, the deferred sequence remains:

1. **FURRY-G00 — Creator & Processor Gate**
2. **FURRY-V01 — Comparative Smoke-Test Design**
3. minimum validation corpus
4. targeted traffic
5. retention/payment measurement
6. kill / iterate / advance

The active Adult Audio workstream returns to **MM Audio Drama vs M4M Roleplay**.
