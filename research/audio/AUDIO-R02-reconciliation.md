# AUDIO-R02 — Independent-pass reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** Canonical reconciliation note  
**Scope:** MM Audio Drama vs M4M Roleplay

## 1. Result

Two independent R02 research passes converged on the same operational decision:

> **Test M4M Roleplay first. Keep MM Audio Drama immediately behind it.**

This is not a claim that M4M is the better long-run business.

The reason is information economics:
- M4M has direct creator-level WTP evidence;
- its central platform uncertainty is narrow and falsifiable;
- proven existing tracks can remove most content-quality confounding;
- MM may have the larger market and stronger IP upside, but a cheap MM pilot is much harder to interpret.

## 2. Shared findings

Both passes support:

- M4M willingness-to-pay exists at meaningful creator scale, including roughly ~1k paid for the strongest verified specialist creator;
- the key unknown is **platform WTP**, not creator WTP;
- **cross-creator consumption** is the decisive behavioral metric;
- MM has a larger visible paid romance/audiobook ecosystem;
- MM production is materially more expensive and quality-sensitive;
- both models become difficult when CAC rises;
- near-free partner-led acquisition is strategically important.

## 3. Changes adopted from the independent pass

### More conservative economics

Canonical model now includes:
- 12% blended tax;
- 12% adult processor + €0.25/txn;
- 3% refunds/chargebacks;
- €1,500 monthly fixed opex;
- age verification across the free-sample funnel;
- CAC sensitivity to €0/10/20/40;
- 58% first-renewal benchmark reality check.

This moves planning away from the casual “2–4k payers” shorthand toward **~3–6k+ payers** under realistic non-zero CAC scenarios.

### Creator gate before build

The original 3-creator / 6-audio concept is superseded.

New sequence:

1. contact 12 creators;
2. ≥5 accept → build;
3. ≤3 accept → kill multi-creator platform version;
4. if built, use 5–6 creators / ~15 already-proven tracks.

This tests creator willingness before software/content spend and reduces the risk that weak bespoke test content contaminates the result.

## 4. Finding retained from the first pass

The first pass identified **Kampsite** as a close historical predecessor: a male-for-male erotic-audio destination/subscription proposition that launched in 2020 and left no visible durable traction.

No reliable post-mortem was found.

Therefore Kampsite is:
- **not** proof the market failed;
- **is** evidence that the idea is not untouched whitespace;
- **is** a reason to demand strong platform-level validation.

## 5. MM factual reconciliation

The independent report said no public performance figures for `Ember & Ice` were available.

That is corrected.

At reconciliation time:
- Quinn Episode Two publicly showed **1,810,926 plays / 90,790 favorites**;
- Vanity Fair reported **>27M listening minutes by 2026-01-19** based on Quinn data.

Interpretation:

- serialized MM erotica can clearly generate very large engagement;
- raw plays/favorites do not establish unique listeners, paid conversion or cohort retention;
- the project was heavily confounded by `Heated Rivalry` celebrity/IP momentum.

So the correction **strengthens MM evidence without changing the first-test ordering**.

## 6. Canonical statuses

- **M4M Roleplay — FIRST VALIDATION CANDIDATE**
- **MM Audio Drama — DEFERRED / TEST SECOND**
- **Furry — HOLD**

“DEFERRED” is intentionally distinct from HOLD. MM remains compelling and should inherit the active slot if M4M fails its gate/test or if a strong author/adaptation partner materially lowers MM validation cost.
